# Bundled third-party assets

- v86 JavaScript/WebAssembly emulator: https://github.com/copy/v86 (BSD-2-Clause; licenses/v86-LICENSE).
- xterm.js and Fit addon: https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js (MIT; licenses/xterm-LICENSE).
- SeaBIOS and VGA BIOS: https://github.com/coreboot/seabios (LGPL-3.0; licenses/seabios-COPYING and COPYING.LESSER).

The files are copied unchanged from the tested local PolyLab baseline. Original upstream build revisions were not recorded when those assets were first downloaded. ASSET-SHA256SUMS identifies the exact vendored files; preserve them to recreate this baseline. Consult upstream source repositories for rebuilding assets. Fonts, marked, and DOMPurify load externally as specified by the HTML template.
